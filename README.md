## ▸ 01 · PROFILE

I design, build, and operate web platforms from the interface down to the pipeline that deploys them. My work sits at the seam between product engineering and infrastructure — shipping React and Node services that are fast for users, and putting them behind CI/CD, containers, and infrastructure-as-code so they stay reliable as teams and traffic grow.

I care about the parts of engineering that survive the first release: clear system boundaries, observable services, reproducible environments, and deployments that are boring by design. I've worked across greenfield builds and inherited codebases, and I'm equally comfortable defining architecture or reducing someone else's 40-minute deploy to four.

Currently deepening: **platform engineering**, **observability**, and **cost-aware cloud architecture**.
